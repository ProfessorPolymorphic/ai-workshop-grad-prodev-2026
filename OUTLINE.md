# Workshop outline

2:30 to 5:00 pm, for Biology and BCB graduate students, with experience ranging from none to advanced. About half the time is hands-on. Students work in **MindRouter** and **Vandal Chat**; the instructor demos **Vandalizer**.

Rough balance: skills and judgment first, the research lifecycle second, tools and logistics the rest.

## Activity 1: Meet your tools (25 min)

| Min | Tool | Students do | Judgment skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | MindRouter | Live status page; sign in; browse the models | Choosing a model; where your data goes |
| 10 | Vandal Chat | Same question, two models, compare with a neighbor. Ask for three papers on your thesis topic; check one DOI | Answers vary between models; sources get invented |
| 9 | Vandalizer (instructor demo) | Watch an extraction from the workshop paper; check each field against the PDF | Evaluating output against the source |
